SLIDE 02 · ABOUT ME @coherence_visual_designer

About Me

My name is Prashant Kumar. I walked into the campus of Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University back in 2022 as a fresh student, and four years later, in 2026, I walked out as a graduate, ready to start my career.

The main reason I am here today is to share the story of how I navigated that crucial gap between being a student and becoming a professional. I'll be talking about how I managed to land my first job offer right in the middle of my final semester.

“I want to extend a heartfelt thank you to all my teachers at DBUUU for believing in me, pushing me to do better, and giving me the confidence to step into the corporate world.”

INSIGHT 01

Scope depends on skill, not just a degree.

The market doesn't care about your mark sheet. Clients and design studios pay for strong visual taste, software agility, and creative problem solving.

INSIGHT 02

Design & branding are in demand everywhere.

From apparel brands in Delhi to tech startups and production houses, every commercial industry requires skilled visual designers.

SLIDE 13 · BLUEPRINT FOR DBUU JUNIORS

Key Lessons for Juniors

01. Start early. Don't wait for the final year.
04. Intern seriously. Even bad ones teach vital lessons.
02. Build portfolio. Start creating from day one.
05. Freelance often. Builds skills, income, and confidence.
03. Embrace failure. It is part of the process.
06. Keep learning. Master new tools and design trends.
