# Prashant Kumar — Alumni Speaker Showcase & Presentation

A warm editorial showcase website and interactive 15-slide presentation deck created for **Prashant Kumar** (BFA 2026, Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University), Visual Designer placed at **J Town Productions**.

Based on the talk: **“My Journey From BFA to Working as a Designer — Failure didn't stop me, it shaped me.”**

---

## 🌟 What's Included

1. **[index.html](file:///c:/Lappy%20Backup/Antigravity/Prashant%20Coherence/college%20frame/index.html)** — Comprehensive Editorial Showcase:
   - **Hero & Homecoming Cover**: Prashant Kumar, BFA Class of 2026, Placed at J Town Productions, Dev Bhoomi Uttarakhand University.
   - **About Me (Slide 2)**: Opening letter detailing walking into DBUU in 2022, graduating in 2026, and heartfelt gratitude to teachers.
   - **Why Fine Arts & Busting the Myth (Slides 3 & 4)**: Deconstructing *“There is no scope in Fine Arts”* with real industry data on skills and commercial demand.
   - **Chronological Journey Timeline (Slides 5–7, 9, 11)**:
     - *Year 1*: Foundations, drawing, color theory, digital shift to Photoshop & Illustrator.
     - *Year 2*: First freelance steps (YouTube thumbnails, reel covers, social media).
     - *The Setback*: First internship failure and what it taught about commercial readiness.
     - *Delhi Chapter*: Bouncing back in Delhi NCR as a T-shirt graphic designer, mastering print-ready specs and tight deadlines.
     - *Years 3–4*: Portfolio from real client work, handling business and pricing.
     - *The Goal*: Hired before graduation at J Town Productions.
     - *Full Circle*: Returning as an invited alumni speaker to DBUU.
   - **Commercial Apparel & T-Shirt Portfolio (Slide 10)**: Real streetwear graphics designed by Prashant (*Skull Tree, Magician TV Head, Egg Topus, Love Yourself, Disturbing Rose, Inner Peace, No Bad Vibe, I DO IT*).
   - **Night Club Logo Case Study (Slide 8)**: Detailed concept breakdowns (Toasting friends + 'R', joy symbol + star, dance circle + vinyl disc).
   - **Campus Placement Spotlight (Slide 12)**: Featuring Prashant's official DBUU `#OurProudAchiever` placement poster.
   - **6 Hard-Won Lessons for Juniors (Slide 13)**: The blueprint for Fine Arts students.
   - **4 Guiding Rules (Slide 14)**: Practical principles to live by.
   - **Connect Hub**: Direct links to `@coherence_visual_designer` on Instagram, LinkedIn, and email.

## 🚀 How to Run & Present

- **Preview the Showcase**: Double-click `index.html` in your browser.
- **Run the Presentation Deck**: Open `presentation.html` and use your keyboard arrow keys (`←` / `→`) to flip slides, or click **Toggle Fullscreen**.
- **Stage Mode on Main Page**: Click **Stage Mode** on `index.html` for an auditorium projection view with keyboard navigation.
